THE POCKET GLOSSARY
Bid / Ask

Highest buyer price / lowest seller price currently quoted.

Spread

The difference between bid and ask; one trading cost.

Liquidity

How easily an asset can be traded without moving its price much.

Slippage

Difference between expected and executed price.

Leverage

Exposure amplified using borrowed funds or derivatives.

Drawdown

Decline from a portfolio's peak value to a later trough.

Stop-loss

An order or instruction intended to limit a losing position.

R-multiple

Trade result expressed as a multiple of initial risk.
